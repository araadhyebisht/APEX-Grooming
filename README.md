# APEX Grooming | APM Lead Gen Project 🚀
**Live Demo:** https://apex-grooming.vercel.app/

## 📌 Project Overview
This repository contains the single-page lead generation website built for the **Associate Product Manager Hiring Challenge** at **Infinite Locus**. 

The objective was to design a targeted landing page from scratch, establish a clear value proposition, and capture user leads into a lightweight database. This project demonstrates a bias toward action, user-centric problem solving, and a lean approach to product validation.

## 🎯 The Product: APEX
APEX is a conceptual premium, toxin-free men's hair styling brand. It is built to solve the specific grooming challenges of modern men, particularly focusing on climate-proof performance (e.g., hot and humid weather) and tailored styling solutions.

* **Target Audience:** Modern, style-conscious men looking for clean, high-performance grooming products tailored to their specific hair types.
* **Core Value Proposition:** Precision styling without the toxins. APEX moves away from the "one-size-fits-all" myth, offering specialized lines for different hair textures, featuring a signature Matte Clay for climate-proof hold, and an upcoming AI Stylist to eliminate product guesswork.

## 🧠 Strategic Product Decisions
To approach this challenge with an APM mindset, I utilized the **Kano Model** to structure the product offering and the page layout:

1. **Performance Needs (The MVP):** * *The Pivot:* Initially conceptualized as a "Universal Fit," I pivoted to a **Precision Range** because a single product contradicts the need for our AI recommender. 
   * *The Core:* 100% clean ingredients and the climate-proof Matte Line. This builds immediate trust and addresses primary user pain points (hair damage and weather-induced styling failure).
2. **The Delighter (The AI Hook):** * Instead of building a complex AI recommendation engine for Day 1, I positioned the "AI Stylist" as an upcoming feature. 
   * *The Strategy:* This acts as a "Fake Door" test and a high-value lead magnet. By tracking sign-ups and interactions with this specific section, we can validate user appetite for AI-driven personalization before investing heavy engineering resources.

## ✨ Key Features & UI/UX
* **Glassmorphic Design:** A premium, aspirational dark-mode aesthetic utilizing frosted glass effects and subtle micro-interactions to appeal to the target demographic.
* **Mobile-Responsive Layout:** Fully optimized for seamless viewing across mobile, tablet, and desktop.
* **Frictionless Lead Capture:** A clean form collecting Name, Email, and Hair Type (to gather valuable initial user data right from the start).
* **Automated Data Storage:** Form submissions are automatically routed and stored in a Google Sheet for easy tracking by sales and marketing teams.
